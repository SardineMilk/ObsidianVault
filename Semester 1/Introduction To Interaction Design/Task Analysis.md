 Hierarchical Task Analysis
- Pros
	- Objectively compare different plans
	- Good understanding of interaction at whatever level of detail
	- Support design reuse at different levels of abstraction
- Cons
	- Can become extremely complex and unwieldy
	- Limited in tasks it can model
		- not parallel
- For example
	- 0 Buy DVD 
		- 0 is the final goal
	- 1 find DVD
	- 2 Add to shopping basket
	- 3 Enter payment details
	- 4 Enter shipping address
	- 5 Confirm order

0 would be above all the other steps in the hierarchy
