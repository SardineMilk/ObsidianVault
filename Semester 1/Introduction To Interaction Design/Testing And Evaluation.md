#### Interviews
Structured
- all set questions
Semi Structured
- Driven by questions
Unstructured
- Driven by topics

Advantages
- can explore very in depth
- relatively cheap

Disadvantages
- can be subjective
- very time consuming

#### Focus Groups
Group discussion interview
People with a particular set of characteristics
Monitored by interviewer
Informal
Open questions

Much better for early stages than later stages

#### Think Aloud Protocols
- User observed performing task
- Describes what and why they're doing

Advantages
- simplicity
- useful insights
- shows how system is really being used

Disadvantages
- Subjective

#### Experimental Research

A/B test with a single variable changing

- General terms
- Independent Variables
	- test conditions
- Dependent variables
	- what you measure
- Confounding variables
	- do not control but can affect measurements
