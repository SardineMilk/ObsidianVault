#### Why is colour choice important 
Colour evoke different emotions, helps to influence the user on brand and overall website. 
#### Choosing colours 
Certain palettes have associative meaning (Red, White, Green - ) Individual attractive colours may not work well in a palette. 
##### Main 3 Aspects
- Complementary
    - Continuity - lower contrast, more gradient-like.
- Contrast
    - Biggest visual impact, clarity is often increased.
- Vibrancy
    - Combination of colours that emits energy, grabs attention.

##### Hues + Tint/Tone/Shade
- Primary - Red, Blue, Yellow
- Secondary - Orange, Green, Purple
- Tertiary - Red-Orange, Yellow-Orange.. etc..

- Tint - +white
- Tone - +grey
- Shade - +black

##### Easy Palettes
- Contrast - Opposite on the colour wheel.
    - Biggest visual impact, clarity is often increased.
- Analogous - Adjacent on colour wheel.
    - Continuity - lower contrast, more gradient-like.
- Triad - 1/3 points on colour wheel.
- Square - 1/4 points on colour wheel.
