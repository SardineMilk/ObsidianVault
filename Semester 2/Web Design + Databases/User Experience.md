User Experience is a result of many factors:
- [[User Interface]]
	- principles
	- technology
- [[Information Architecture]] 
- Human-Computer Interaction

Norman doors are examples of good/bad UX
### Components
#### Accessibility
All people can use the website, including disabled/elderly
#### Usability
Ease of using software
Achieve goals effectively and efficiently
#### Reliability
Website will perform reliably
Consistent results under stated conditions
#### Efficiency
How quickly can tasks be accomplished
Users complete tasks without wasting time, resources or energy
#### Attractiveness
Visually appealing website
Interesting features/qualities
#### Meaningfulness
Human ability to convert input data -> useful information 
Can users convert information on site to their own knowledge
