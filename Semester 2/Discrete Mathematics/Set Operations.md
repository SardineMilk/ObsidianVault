"$:=$" means "equal by definition"

$A \cup B$ 
- Union of A and B
- $A \cup B := \{x | x \in A \space or \space x \in B\}$

$A \cap B$
- Intersection of A and B
- $A \cap B := \{x | x \in A \space and \space x \in B\}$

$A \textbackslash B$ 
- Difference of A and B
- $A \textbackslash B := \{x|x  \in A \space and \space x \notin B\}$
- Not commutative: $A \textbackslash B \not = B \textbackslash A$ (generally)

$\bar A$
- Complement of set A
- $\bar A = U \textbackslash A$
- $\bar A = \{x|x \notin A\}$

#### Laws
$A \cup (B \cup C) = (A \cup B) \cup C$
$A \cap (B \cap C) = (A \cap B) \cap C$
$A \cup B = B \cup A$
$A \cap B = B \cap A$
$A \cup (B \cap C) = (A \cup B) \cap (A \cup C)$
$A \cap (B \cup C) = (A \cap B) \cup (A \cap C)$
$\overline {(A \cup B)} = \bar A \cap \bar B$
$\overline {(A \cap B)} = \bar A \cup \bar B$
