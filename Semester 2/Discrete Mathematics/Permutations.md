

The number of *unordered* subsets of the set $n$ of length $k$ is:
$\frac{n!}{(n-k)! * k}$

# $\binom{n}{k} := \frac{n!}{(n-k)! * k!}$
"$n$ choose $k$"

**Example:**
How many possible 13 card bridge hands are in a 52 card deck?
$n = 52$
$k = 13$
$\binom{52}{13} := \frac{52!}{(52-13)! * 13!}$
$=635,013,559,600$
