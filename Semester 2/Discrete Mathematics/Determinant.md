$$det(\begin{bmatrix}
a & b \\
c & d
\end{bmatrix}
) = ad - bc$$

Imagine a linear transformation of a [[Vector Spaces|vector space]]
The determinant is the factor that the area of a unit square is altered by the transformation
If the orientation is flipped, it is negative

**Example**
$$
A =\begin{bmatrix}
2 & 0 \\
0 & 2
\end{bmatrix}
$$
$A$ scales everything by a factor of 2 in both the $x$ and $y$ dimensions
It has a determinant of $2*2 - 0*0 = 4$


$$
B =\begin{bmatrix}
-5 & 0 \\
0 & 5
\end{bmatrix}
$$
$B$ scales both $x$ and $y$ by 5, and inverts $x$
It has a determinant of $-5*5 - 0%0 = -25$

\end{bmatrix}
$$
$C$ squishes everything down to a single line

It has a determinant of $4*1 - 2*2 = 0$
