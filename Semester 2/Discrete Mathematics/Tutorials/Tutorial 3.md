9.
How many integers from 1 to 500 are divisible by 4 or by 6?
$|A|$ = number divisible by 4
$= 500 / 4 = 125$
$|B|$ = number divisible by 6
$= 500 / 6 = 83$ (integer part)

$|A \cap B|$ = number divisible by 12 (LCM)
$= 41$

$|A \cup B| = |A| + |B| - |A \cap B|$
$= 125 + 83 - 41 = 167$
