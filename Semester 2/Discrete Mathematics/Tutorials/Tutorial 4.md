An experiment consists of 3 consecutive throws of a die
Each outcome is equally probably
Find:

a)
the probability of three 6's

Let A be the event that a single roll results in a 6
$P(A) = 1/6$
Therefore the probability of three 6's:
$1/6 * 1/6 * 1/6 = 1/216$

b)
At least one 6

Let $A$ be the event of *not* rolling at least one 6
$P(A) = 5/6 * 5/6 * 5/6$
$P(A) = 125/216$
The probability of rolling a 6 is:
$1 - P(A) = 1 - 125/216 = 91/216$

c)
The probability of two consecutive 6's

In roll 1+2
$1/6 * 1/6 = 1/36$

In roll 2+3
$1/6 * 1/6 = 1/36$

Inclusion-Exclusion:
6,6,6 was counted twice
$1/216$

Result:
$1/36 + 1/36 - 1/216$
$= 11/216$
