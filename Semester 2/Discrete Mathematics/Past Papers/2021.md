1. *10/10*
Prove $5|6^n - 1$ for any integer $n \geq 1$

*Step 1: prove base case*
$5|6^1 - 1$
$5|6-1$
$5|5$ 
This is true, so the statement holds for $n=1$

*Step 2: Induction step*
Assume $5|6^k - 1$ holds for any natural number $k$
So there is an integer $m$ such that
$5m = 6^k - 1$

We must show the statement holds for $k+1$
$6^{k+1} - 1$
$= 6(6^k) - 1$
$= 6(6^k - 1) + 5$
$= 6(5m) + 5$
$= 30m + 5$
$= 5(6m + 1)$

$m$ is an integer, therefore $5|6^{k+1} - 1$ follows from $5|6^k - 1$

By the principle of mathematical induction, the statement holds for any natural number $n$
