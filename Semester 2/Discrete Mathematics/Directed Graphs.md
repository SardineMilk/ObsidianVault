Directed Graphs / Digraphs

$G(V, E)$
$V$ - set of vertices
$E$ - list of ordered pairs of vertices (directed edges)

The [[Adjacency Matrix]] of a digraph with $n$ vertices is a $nxn$ matrix with entries
$a_{ij} =$ the number of directed edges from vertex $v_i$ to vertex $v_j$
